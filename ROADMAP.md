# XDownloader — Roadmap

## v1.0 — Shipped

All core features are live. See [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md) for the full list.

---

## v1.x — Near-term

- **Finish notification** — post a macOS notification when a download completes
- **Batch input** — paste or import multiple URLs at once and queue them all
- **Menu bar icon** — live download count badge in the menu bar

---

## v2.0 — Future

- **In-app preview** — thumbnail or inline video player for completed downloads
- **Per-download format** — override the global format setting on a per-URL basis
- **Share Sheet / browser extension** — send URLs to XDownloader directly from Safari or Chrome
- **Import from file** — load a plain-text list of URLs and download them all
